Retracing Odysseus's Wake

No one agrees where any of this really happened. For as long as people have told this story, they've also argued about the map underneath it — this coast or that one, this island or its neighbour. What follows are the versions most often told, not a verdict. Scroll, and the map will retrace the ten-year detour between a war's end and a man's front door.
